🔒 Privacy Policy (English)

This Privacy Policy explains how RentLedger Pro ("we", "our", or "app") processes information when you use our mobile application on Android.

Privacy First Guarantee: RentLedger Pro is designed with an offline-first architecture. All property data, tenant names, lease details, and rent collection records are stored locally on your device via SQLite. We do NOT operate remote databases collecting or selling your private rental records.

1. Information We Collect & Process

  • Local Data Storage: Property names, tenant names, phone numbers, rent amounts, payment dates, and landlord profile headers (including optional IBAN) are stored strictly within the app's local SQLite database on your device.
  • Advertising ID (Google AdMob): For non-Pro users, the app integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display non-personalized and personalized banner or rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising ID (AD_ID), IP address, and general diagnostic logs in compliance with Google Play Policy.
  • In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (RevenueCat & Google Play): We use RevenueCat and Google Play In-App Billing to process Pro subscriptions and one-time purchases safely. Financial transactions are securely processed by Google Play. We never have access to your credit card or banking details.

2. Device Permissions

  • INTERNET: Required for loading AdMob ads, verifying active subscriptions via RevenueCat, and backing up data if selected.
  • BILLING: Required for processing Google Play Pro in-app subscriptions and lifetime purchases.
  • AD_ID: Declared in compliance with Android 13+ requirements for ad services.
