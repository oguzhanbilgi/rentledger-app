# RentLedger Pro — Privacy Policy & Terms of Service
*Last Updated: October 2026*

## English

### 1. Overview
**RentLedger Pro** is an offline-first property and rent payment tracking application designed for landlords and property managers.

### 2. Data Storage & Privacy Guarantee
- **Local Storage:** All property records, tenant names, contact details, rent amounts, payment histories, and landlord profile headers (including IBAN) are stored strictly inside the local SQLite database on your device.
- We do **not** transmit, collect, or store your personal tenancy or financial data on external servers.

### 3. Third-Party Services
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** For non-Pro users, the app integrates AdMob to display banner and rewarded ads. AdMob processes standard device identifiers (such as `AD_ID`) and technical telemetry in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.
- **RevenueCat & Google Play In-App Billing:** Pro subscriptions and purchases are processed directly and securely through Google Play. We do not collect or store credit card details.

### 4. Permissions
- `INTERNET`: Required for loading ads and verifying subscription status.
- `BILLING`: Required for Google Play in-app purchases.
- `AD_ID`: Declared for Google AdMob advertising identifier support on Android 13+.
